## ASCII / Borradura

The origin is operational before it is aesthetic. The dossier
`projects/cultura/borradura_ascii/DOSSIER.md` records that ASCII-only was a
Windows checkout/encoding defense, later applied beyond its safe scope. The
concrete failure was Spanish output such as `disenio` and `ano`, where the
second word is not merely ugly but a different word.

The art converts the safety rule into an instrument:

- `projects/cultura/borradura_ascii/borradura.py` measures losses;
- `projects/cultura/borradura_ascii/tapiz.py` renders the erased marks;
- `projects/cultura/borradura_ascii/tapiz.txt` preserves a readable material
  trace;
- `iskvw/piel/campo/ASCII_REFERENCIA.md` records the later ASCII-as-pattern
  technique: sample the vector form as glyph texture instead of drawing only
  its contour.

Disposition: real technical scar → measurement → cultural piece → public
technique candidate. It is not a general language policy; UTF-8 remains the
correct contract for human-facing material.

## Tilde

The rainstorm starts Tilde with Spanish ambiguity, literature as representation
and managed translation loss. The dossier then becomes:

- `desktop/tilde_meter.py`: measures survival of diacritics through compression;
- `projects/tilde/sobrevivencia.py`: renders the sediment of that corpus;
- `projects/cultura/tilde_residuo.py` and `tilde_paridad.py`: formalize residue
  and parity rather than treating loss as a cosmetic defect;
- `projects/tapiz/TILDE_DEL_HIGH.md`: extends the same structure to the felt
  state that language can describe but an LLM cannot experience.

The named piece `VOLÁ` makes the operation self-demonstrating: the accent
changes the addressee/meaning, so the title itself carries its own residue.

## Psicosis

Psicosis begins as an epistemic question, not a clinical classifier: input a
situation, study how it has been recorded, and keep open the possibility that
the record is wrong. The agent dossier documents a real 2026 failure where a
defensive rule rejected legitimate instructions; that incident became material
in `projects/cultura/psicosis_agente/`.

The concept was then made structural rather than diagnostic:

- `fila_cero.py` makes author/reader asymmetry a data-model property;
- `mecanismo_residuo.py` separates shared output from incompatible reasons;
- `cartografia_filtros/` and related dossiers preserve the study/indexing
  direction without profiling real people.

Disposition: cultural epistemology and instrument prototypes; never a tool for
surveillance or diagnosing third parties.

## Tapiz and Cauce

Tapiz starts from carpets as cultural pattern, domestic warmth and psychedelic
visual experience. Its code translation is the `projects/tapiz/vibecode/`
package: code generation becomes a woven pattern, with whitespace as material.

`cauce.py` is not a separate unrelated project; it is the derived mode where
recurring tokens become threads, vertical whitespace becomes rivers and
non-ASCII marks survive as bright cultural marks. The `cauce` pieces and the
3D sala are later materializations of that same recognition/negative-space
operation.

`DIRECTION.md`, `VOLA.md` and the curated SVGs establish the figure/ground
relationship: visible double cup versus invisible code void, human perception
versus static agent reading. The measure (low luminance delta) is the cultural
regularizer that prevents the idea from becoming an unverified visual claim.

## Precursor

Precursor is the descriptive research line about drug design, designer-drug
history, seed/phenotype culture and patents/Markush structures as visual/legal
artifacts. The active README classifies it as a dossier/stub rather than a
finished operational tool. It is a cultural lens, not a synthesis or production
pipeline.

## Unified reading

```text
technical scar / research question
  -> dossier and source evidence
  -> instrument that makes the loss, pattern or asymmetry measurable
  -> material (SVG, text, ledger, viewer, dossier)
  -> piece or safe public portfolio projection
```

indexing/provenance architecture while remaining different products. A venue
3D model, a chaotic-folder index and a plano/rider all turn hidden structure
into a usable representation; their consumers and safety contracts differ.
